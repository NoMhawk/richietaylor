About Richie Taylor

I’m Richie Taylor, a life coach who’s been through the grinder and come out sharper.

Based in Yeovil, UK, I’ve helped dozens of people, from stuck professionals to lost dreamers, face their realities head-on. My style is direct. If you’re avoiding the hard stuff, we’ll fix that first. I believe real growth comes from uncomfortable truths, not motivational posters.

Whether you’re in Somerset or across the globe, I deliver results without the fluff. Let’s cut through the noise and get you moving.

Who this is for

I’m direct, and that doesn’t suit everyone. Better to find out now.

A good fit

  • You know what needs to change and keep putting it off.

Not a good fit
