How this works

  • At about 06:00 and 18:00 UTC each day, a scheduled job saves exactly what the forecast page shows for 08:00-18:00 over the next 7 days.
  • Every 3 hours it collects the actual weather reports (METARs) from Exeter (EGTE) and Yeovilton (EGDY). Dunkeswell has no report of its own, so each forecast hour is checked against both airports.
  • Airport cloud heights are converted to feet above Dunkeswell (833 ft). "Cloudbase" compares RichBot's base with the airport's lowest layer of scattered cloud or more (3+ oktas).
  • "Jumpable" uses the page's rules: wind or gusts over 23 mph, rain, fog, visibility under 3 km, a ceiling under 2,000 ft or overcast cloud = not jumpable.
  • Gusts are only compared when the airport reports a gust (airports only report gusts when they are notable).
  • Bias = forecast minus actual. A positive wind bias means RichBot forecast stronger wind than happened.
  • Each week a review agent reads these results and may make small tuning changes (model weights and bias corrections only). Its reports are listed below.
