How this is worked out
  • At about 06:00 and 18:00 UTC each day, a scheduled job saves exactly what the forecast page shows for 08:00-18:00 over the next 7 days.
  • Every 3 hours it collects the actual weather reports (METARs) from Exeter (EGTE) and Yeovilton (EGDY). Dunkeswell has no report of its own, so each forecast hour is checked against both airports.
  • Airport cloud heights are converted to feet above Dunkeswell (833 ft). "Cloudbase" compares RichBot's base with the airport's lowest layer of scattered cloud or more (3+ oktas).
  • "Jumpable" uses the page's rules: wind or gusts over 23 mph, rain, fog, visibility under 3 km, a base under 2,000 ft or very heavy cloud = not jumpable.
  • The day-by-day list uses the morning forecast for that day (or the closest one before it).
  • Exeter and Yeovilton are lower and more sheltered than Dunkeswell, so the hilltop is usually a bit windier than the airports report.
  • Every morning a review agent reads these results and writes a short report. Once there are 14 days of data it may make small tuning changes (model weights and cloudbase corrections only, at most one a week).
