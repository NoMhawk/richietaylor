Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026.

Who I am

I’m Richie Taylor, trading as The Brutal Truth Life Coaching, based in Yeovil, Somerset, UK. I’m responsible for the personal information you send me through this site. You can reach me at lifecoaching@richietaylor.net.

What I collect

  • The details you type into the contact form: your name, email address, how you’d like to work, and your message.
  • Anything you send me by email or WhatsApp.

I don’t use analytics or advertising cookies on this site.

Why I use it

I use your details to reply to your enquiry and, if we decide to work together, to arrange and run sessions. I don’t sell your information or use it for marketing you haven’t asked for.

Who else handles it

  • The contact form is delivered by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which passes your message to my email inbox.
  • The site’s fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google’s servers when you visit.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages.

How long I keep it

I keep enquiries only as long as I need them to reply and, if we work together, to run our sessions. You can ask me to delete them sooner.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see the information I hold about you, have it corrected, or have it deleted. Email me and I’ll deal with it. If you’re unhappy with how I’ve handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
